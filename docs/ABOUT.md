# About BLACK OCTOBER 2026

**Author / Publisher:** DRACXX (GitHub: DR13X)  
**Type:** Technical research publication  
**Period covered:** 1–31 October 2026  
**Edition:** Expanded Edition (Version 2.1) — 108 pages

## Purpose

Document and analyse the major cybersecurity events of October 2026 with clear evidence labels, plain-English explanations, step-by-step diagrams, and technical depth suitable for SOC analysts, researchers, and security leadership.

## Evidence policy

The book uses explicit evidence labels (Vendor-confirmed, Independently verified, etc.) and states "not established" where sources do not support a claim. It does not invent root causes or exploitation methods.
